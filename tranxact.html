Tranxact.

Tranxact lets people in Nigeria receive crypto and get naira, send to any bank, pay bills and get paid with a link. The previous experience felt busy and technical. The goal was to make money feel simple enough to trust at a glance.

We redesigned the identity around a single mark that smiles — a curve over a solid form — and let purple become the one accent in the product. The app centres on one calm purple card. Everything else stays within reach.
